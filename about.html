About The Dating Edge

Attachment Psychology for Modern Relationships

Our Mission

Psychology-based relationship advice for people who want to understand why patterns repeat - and how to change them.

The Author

Dr. Priya Sharma — Clinical Psychologist, Relationships Specialist

After a decade in clinical practice working with couples and individuals, I got tired of watching people repeat the same patterns while reading the same surface-level dating advice.

The Dating Edge is built around attachment theory, communication research, and the psychology of attraction - not pickup tactics or generic 'love yourself first' platitudes.

Editorial Standards

Every article on The Dating Edge is grounded in peer-reviewed psychology and relationship research. Opinion pieces are clearly labeled. We do not accept sponsored content that conflicts with our editorial independence.
