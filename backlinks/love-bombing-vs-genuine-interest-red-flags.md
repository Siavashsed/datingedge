## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How can you tell the difference between someone who is genuinely interested in you and someone who is love bombing?

This is one of the most important distinctions in modern dating, and the answer lies in understanding how these two approaches differ psychologically.

Genuine interest respects your autonomy and timeline. Someone truly interested in you will:

- Invest time gradually, without pressure to accelerate milestones
- Respect your existing relationships and commitments
- Allow you space to make decisions without manipulation
- Show consistent behavior over time, not just in the honeymoon phase
- Demonstrate interest in who you actually are, not who they imagine you to be

Love bombing, conversely, feels like destiny but operates on a different mechanism:

- Everything happens at an accelerated pace—declarations of love within weeks, meeting family immediately, discussing moving in together
- There's an intensity that feels overwhelming in a flattering way
- The person mirrors your dreams and desires back to you so perfectly it feels fated
- Decision pressure is subtle but real—you're expected to reciprocate the intensity
- Once they've established emotional investment, the behavior often shifts

The key difference: genuine interest builds trust slowly through consistency. Love bombing creates the illusion of connection through intensity.

One crucial signal? If you feel both excited and anxious—like something is moving too fast even though it feels wonderful—trust that instinct. Healthy relationships don't require you to move at someone else's pace to prove your feelings.
